**Anthropic just shipped the first AI install path that doesn't need IT.**

On May 13, Anthropic released Claude for Small Business — a one-toggle install that wires Claude directly into QuickBooks, DocuSign, HubSpot, Canva, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, and PayPal. No integration project, no SSO ticket, no MSP scoping call.

For a 50-person mechanical sub, that list is roughly your entire back-office stack.

Three workflows worth running on a Monday morning:

**1. Subcontract + change-order routing through DocuSign.**
Pull the sub names and amounts from the SOV, draft the cover letter, set the routing order, send for signature. The drafting time for a 12-sub package drops from a couple of hours to a careful review pass. Critical sub commitments still get human eyes on the rate and the scope language — but the typing and routing become AI work.

**2. AR follow-ups through QuickBooks.**
Pull the aging report, segment by GC, draft the "where are we on Application #14" email to each AP contact, suggest who to call. A controller running this every Friday recovers hours and stops letting 60+ day invoices age into 90.

**3. CRM hygiene before a bid open house.**
HubSpot deduplication, owner-rep last-touch notes, a one-paragraph brief on each invited GC pulled from your last three jobs with them. The bid morning where you walk in already knowing which superintendent ran the last Towson Mechanical job and what got disputed.

**What it doesn't touch.**
Procore, Sage 300 CRE, Foundation, Trimble Estimation, Bluebeam. The construction-specific layer is still a custom integration. This is back-office AI, not job-cost AI. Don't expect it to read a spec book.

**What's actually new.**
Every prior "AI for SMB" pitch needed someone to wire it up. This is the first one a 30-person plumbing shop's owner can install on a Sunday night and use Monday morning. The friction floor for trying AI inside a contracting business just dropped to zero.

If your 2026 plan still has "evaluate AI" as a Q3 item, that quarter ended this week.
