## Main post

A year ago, 17% of US contractors said AI tools had moved a real number on their P&L.

This year: 38%.

The COAA's 2026 survey just put a chart on something most of us have been feeling at the bid table — and the supporting numbers are sharp: 82% of large GCs increasing AI budgets, 94% of mid-sized firms implementing or evaluating.
