A year ago, 17% of US contractors said AI tools had moved a real number on their P&L. As of the COAA's 2026 survey, that number is 38% — a doubling in 12 months.

The companion stats from the same survey: 82% of large GCs are increasing AI budgets this year, and 94% of mid-sized firms are either implementing or actively evaluating.

The bid-table math is the part most operators still haven't internalized. When the GC across town can run takeoff in 90 minutes instead of 14 hours, the labor cost in their bid is structurally lower than yours. That margin is yours to lose.
