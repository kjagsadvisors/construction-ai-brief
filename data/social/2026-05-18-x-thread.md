## Tweet 1 (hook)

A year ago, 17% of US contractors said AI tools had moved a real number on their P&L.

This year: 38%.

The COAA's 2026 survey just put a chart on something most of us have been feeling at the bid table.

## Tweet 2 (companion stats)

The supporting numbers from the same survey (N ≈ 350 GCs):

— 82% of large GCs increasing AI budgets in 2026
— 94% of mid-sized firms implementing or actively evaluating
— Adoption is no longer just megaprojects and CIO-heavy shops

## Tweet 3 (bid-table math)

Why this matters for your bid table:

When the GC across town can run a takeoff in 90 minutes instead of 14 hours and auto-route submittals against the spec, the labor cost in their bid is structurally lower than yours.

That margin is yours to lose.

2. AGC's next workforce survey. If the 500K worker shortfall holds, the question flips from "should we adopt" to "we have no choice."
