Anthropic shipped Claude for Small Business on May 13 — a one-toggle install that wires Claude into QuickBooks, DocuSign, HubSpot, Canva, Google Workspace, and Microsoft 365.

For a 50-person mechanical sub, that's roughly the entire back-office stack. No SSO ticket, no MSP scoping call, no integration project. The owner of a 30-person plumbing shop can install it on Sunday night and use it Monday morning.

What it actually does on day one: drafts subcontract and change-order packages through DocuSign, drafts AR follow-up emails from the QuickBooks aging report, and cleans up the HubSpot CRM before a bid open house.

What it doesn't touch: Procore, Sage 300 CRE, Foundation, Trimble Estimation. The construction-specific stack still needs custom wiring.
