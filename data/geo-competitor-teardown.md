## The one-sentence strategy
Stop competing on volume; re-architect for **extraction** (answer-first + question H2s + tables + FAQ schema + llms.txt), own the winnable **question/long-tail** and **"best tools"** clusters now, and manufacture entity authority through **original data** — then the head term "AI for construction" becomes reachable in months, not never.

## Three citation mechanisms in the "AI for construction" AI Overview
1. **Definitional/listicle content** (OpenAsset, Autodesk, ClickUp, STACK) — cited because pages are *structured for extraction*. **Primary winnable lane.**
2. **Entity/earned-media authority** (Trunk Tools, constructionai.io) — cited because CNBC/ENR/BI covered their funding. Not directly winnable; piggyback via fast explainers.
3. **Creator/video authority** (Tim Fairley/ConstructIQ) — named-expert + YouTube. Only via a **brand-fronted** channel (never Keeran by name, per memory rules).

## Query clusters (by winnability)
- **Cluster C — question/decision long-tail — WINNABLE NOW.** "Will AI replace estimators?", "How does AI help with takeoffs?", "How to use ChatGPT for RFIs", "Is AI worth it for a mid-market GC?". Owned by beatable mid-tier sites (ConstructConnect, STACK). AI lifts the direct yes/no answer sentence.
- **Cluster B — "best AI tools for construction 2026" — WINNABLE + high value.** Owned by generic listicles with no opinion. Comparison **tables** win. Our independent "honest tradeoffs" voice is the differentiator.
- **Cluster D — news/stats — home-field.** AI cites specific numbers. Original data or fastest clear explainer wins.
- **Cluster A — head/definitional ("AI in construction") — build now, cite in months.** Needs a definitive pillar + authority.

## Why the site is currently uncitable (structural, not content)
No answer-first structure · no question H2s · no FAQ/FAQPage schema · no comparison tables · no llms.txt · no evergreen pillar/glossary · near-zero backlinks.

## Prioritized actions
**Tier 1 (2–4 wks):**
1. Ship 6–10 answer-first **question pages** (Cluster C): H1 = the question; first 2–3 sentences = a direct quotable answer; question H2s; a pros/cons **table**; a 4–6 item **FAQ block**.
2. **Schema + `llms.txt` sitewide:** Article + Organization + Author + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList; publish `/llms.txt` (kjags allowed there per memory). Retrofit top news posts with a TL;DR answer box + 2–3 question H2s.
3. Flagship **"Best AI tools for construction (2026): an independent field review"** — big comparison table, honest tradeoffs, evergreen, updated quarterly.

**Tier 2 (4–10 wks):**
4. Definitional **pillar page** "AI in construction: what it is and how contractors actually use it" (~3.5–4.5k words, OpenAsset architecture), internally linked to the question pages.
5. **Construction-AI glossary** (30–50 terms) — citation magnet; each term is a definitional answer.
6. **Original data** — one small subscriber survey ("What AI tools are mid-market GCs actually paying for in 2026?") published as a linkable stat page. Highest-leverage GEO move for a small publisher.

**Tier 3 (months 2–6):** entity corroboration (directory listings, "best construction newsletters" roundups, 5–10 contextual backlinks off the data page); fast answer-first explainers on funding/feature news; evaluate a brand-fronted YouTube channel.

## Our advantages to lean into
Editorial independence + opinion (vendors can't say "waste of money"); cross-domain synthesis (macro AI news → construction); publishing velocity for freshness citations.
