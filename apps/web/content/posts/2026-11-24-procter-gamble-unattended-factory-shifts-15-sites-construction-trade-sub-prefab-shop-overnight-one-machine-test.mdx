Procter & Gamble now runs factory shifts with nobody on the floor at 15 sites and plans to add more next year. For a mechanical or electrical sub with a prefab shop, the takeaway is not to buy a robot. It is that unattended hours start with one machine, one well-prepared job, and someone who can see it from home.

## What did P&G announce?

P&G's chief product supply officer, Luc Reynaert, told investors in Cincinnati on November 19 that the company is "taking unattended operations to a whole new level." The New York Times reported the details on November 22, and [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/news/artificial-intelligence/2026/pg-steps-up-use-of-fully-autonomous-factory-shifts/) and the [Hawaii Tribune-Herald's wire brief](https://www.hawaiitribune-herald.com/2026/11/23/nation-world-news/news-in-brief-for-november-23/) carried them:

| What P&G said | The number |
|---|---|
| Sites running shifts without people today | 15, with more planned for 2027 |
| How long lines run unattended | Often overnight, several hours at a time |
| Expected productivity gain from unattended operations | Up to 20% over about four years |
| Productivity savings target | Up to $10 billion, fiscal 2027 to 2030 |
| Product supply workforce reduction target | 20% to 30%, including contractors, by 2030 |

The workforce figure comes from investor-day coverage summarized by [MLQ](https://mlq.ai/news/procter-gamble-plans-to-cut-up-to-30-of-its-product-supply-workforce-by-2030-as-it-runs-more-factory-shifts-without-people/), which notes P&G gave no headcount, plant list or split between attrition and layoffs. P&G also uses robots at loading docks and in warehouses and has tested driverless trucks.

## What does this mean for a sub's prefab shop?

Two details matter more than the $10 billion.

1. **"Unattended" means hours, not days.** The reporting describes lines running overnight for several hours at a time. That is a long, repeatable job with no one standing at the machine. It is not a dark factory that runs itself for a week.
2. **P&G says it depends on skilled people.** Reynaert said success "heavily depends on a workforce with the skills to scale and optimize these technologies," per the MLQ summary. The people move to setup, programming and fixing problems. They don't go away.

A fab shop already has the closest thing to a P&G line: a machine that runs a long job from a file. Pipe cutting and beveling, sheet metal on a CNC table, a band saw with a bar feeder, threading runs. Many of those sit idle from the end of second shift until morning. Shops chasing more output without more hires should start there.

## What does an overnight test run need?

Before anyone in the shop talks about AI or robots, check whether one machine can finish one job overnight with nobody there:

- **Material staged for the whole run.** If someone has to reload stock halfway through, it is not unattended.
- **The job file checked before everyone leaves.** Cut lists or nests checked against the current spool drawings, because nobody will catch a revision mismatch at 2 a.m.
- **A way to see it remotely.** A camera on the machine and an alert when it faults or stops. Ask your dealer whether your control can send that alert.
- **A defined stop condition.** What the machine does on a fault, and who gets the call.
- **A sign-off from your insurer and fire marshal.** Leaving thermal cutting equipment running with nobody in the building is a coverage question first. Ask before the first run, not after.
- **A morning check.** Measure the first and last parts off the run against the drawing before they go to the field.

Track run hours, scrap and morning rework for a month. If one machine adds real hours with no rework, you have a case for a second. If the morning crew spends the first hour cleaning up the night run, you've found out cheaply.

## What are the limits?

P&G makes the same products in huge volumes. A fab shop's work changes job to job and spool to spool, which is the hardest case for unattended runs. P&G hasn't said what equipment or software its lines use, so nobody should read this as a product recommendation. The $10 billion target also covers more than unattended shifts, and outlets describe the savings figures in slightly different ways.

We covered the robot side of shop automation when [OpenAI folded its robotics team](/posts/2026-11-16-openai-folds-robotics-team-general-models-run-robots-construction-trade-sub-prefab-shop-robot-quote-latency-token-cost). The P&G story suggests a step before that: get unattended hours out of the machines you already own.

**Takeaway:** Pick the one machine in your shop that runs the longest jobs from a file. Run it unattended for one overnight job this month, with material staged, the file checked, a camera on it and your insurer's OK. Measure the morning rework before you price anything new.
