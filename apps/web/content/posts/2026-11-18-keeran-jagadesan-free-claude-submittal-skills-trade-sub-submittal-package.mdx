Submittals are where a trade sub's office hours go to die: pull the spec section, build the log, chase every cut sheet, check each one against the spec, stamp it, bind it, send it. Keeran Jagadesan, the AI creator who posts as @keeran.ai, sat down with a real plumbing job and did the whole run on camera with Claude, start to finish, in a video that runs just under 12 minutes.

It's one of the more useful pieces of AI-for-construction content we've seen this year, for a simple reason: it's a real job, not a demo deck, and the tools he used are a free download.

## What does the workflow actually do?

From the video's chapters, the run goes like this:

- **Setup.** Install the Claude desktop app, open Cowork, and add the submittal skills. This part takes a couple of minutes.
- **Organize the cut-sheet library.** Point Claude at a messy folder of product data and let it sort and name what's there.
- **Build the submittal log from the spec.** Claude reads the spec section and generates the log of what has to be submitted.
- **Build the package.** Each log item gets matched to a cut sheet and assembled into one package.
- **Catch non-compliant items.** Products that don't meet the spec get flagged instead of slipping through.
- **Fill the gaps.** Missing cut sheets get sourced from the manufacturer.
- **Mark up the cut sheets.** Highlights on the specified model and a review stamp, the part most offices still do by hand in Bluebeam.

## What it can't do

- **It doesn't replace the reviewer.** A flag is a prompt to look, not a decision. Substitutions, "or equal" calls and anything the spec leaves to judgment still need someone who knows the job.
- **It's one trade on one job.** The demo is plumbing. The skills are built to work across trades, but your division's quirks are yours to test.
- **Garbage cut sheets in, garbage package out.** If the product data on file is outdated, Claude will package outdated product data, neatly.

If your PMs are building submittal packages by hand, yes. The cost of finding out is one afternoon and nothing out of pocket: watch the [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gIvSt4LlweU), grab the [free skills](https://kjagsadvisors.com/submittal-skills), and run one closed-out job you already know cold.
