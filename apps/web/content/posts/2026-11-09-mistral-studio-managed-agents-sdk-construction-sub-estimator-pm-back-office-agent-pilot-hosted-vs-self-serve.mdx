**Mistral has opened a public preview of tools for building and running business agents, and a trade sub can test it on one low-risk back-office task today. Private-cloud hosting, the part most contractors would want for bid and pricing data, is not live yet.** The preview is not a contractor product. It is a toolkit, and what it does for you depends on who builds with it.

## What did Mistral actually release?

According to coverage of the November 5 launch, the preview includes:

- **Managed agents**, which run multi-step jobs on Mistral-hosted infrastructure.
- **An Agents SDK**, a Python package. Developers test locally, then either self-host with `agents serve` or hand the agent to Mistral with `agents deploy`.
- **A visual workflow builder**, where you describe an automation in plain language and the tool generates code you can inspect and edit.
- **Evaluation tools**, for checking whether an agent does the job correctly.

Mistral's own docs label Workflows a public preview, and some outlets disagree on what is generally available. Expect changes.

## What would a trade sub or estimator use it for?

Think of the paperwork that eats a coordinator's week, not the work that carries risk:

| Task | Fit for a first test | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Sorting submittal and RFI emails into a log | Good | Easy to check against the originals |
| Drafting a weekly status email for a PM to approve | Good | A human reads it before it goes out |
| Matching delivery tickets to purchase orders | Maybe | Needs clean data and a review step |
| Building bid pricing from takeoff files | Not yet | Wrong output costs real money, and the data is sensitive |
| Sending pay apps or change order language | No | Contract and payment risk |

## Should data stay on your own servers?

For now, ask before you upload. Reporting says private-cloud and on-premises installs are expected in late November and sit in the Enterprise tier at a negotiated price. Most firms of 20 to 200 people will not buy that. The practical path is to test with sample or already-public documents and read the data terms before connecting a real inbox.

## Does a mid-size sub need this now?

Probably not as a purchase. The named production customers in the coverage are large banks and consulting firms, not contractors. If you have a person in the office who can write a little Python, or a consultant who can, a two-week test costs little. If you do not, wait for the tools built on top of platforms like this.

## The takeaway

Choose one task that someone on your team does weekly and dislikes. Write down how long it takes and how often it goes wrong. Run the preview on sample files, have a person check every output, and compare. If it saves time without new errors, you have a reason to keep going. If not, you spent an afternoon, and you will know what to ask vendors who build on agent platforms like this one.
