If your precon team runs spec reviews, quote leveling, or homemade Codex tools on a paid ChatGPT plan, you now get less GPT-6.2 work per plan than you did last week. OpenAI says a billing error had been under-counting usage of GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol. With the fix, OpenAI says users hit their limits 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol. The practical move for an estimator is simple: check your allowance before the next bid, and don't spend the one free reset OpenAI is handing out until a deadline actually needs it.

## What did OpenAI change?

According to [heise online](https://www.heise.de/en/news/OpenAI-corrects-billing-error-GPT-6-2-limits-arrive-sooner-11539518.html), Thibault Sottiaux of OpenAI disclosed on November 26 that a billing error had "incorrectly discounted" Astra and Sol usage "for most users" since the models launched on November 17. [Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/ChatGPT-users-hit-limits-up-to-40-sooner-after-OpenAI-billing-fix.1460643.0.html) and [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/article/gpt-6-2-billing-fix-astra-sol/) report the same figures.

The key points:

- **Limits arrive sooner:** 40% faster on Astra and 25% faster on Sol than the week before. Other models were not affected.
- **No back-billing:** OpenAI says no one will be charged for the usage it missed.
- **One banked reset:** Go, Plus, Pro and Business users get a one-time reset of their allowance, to use whenever they choose.
- **Still better than before, per OpenAI:** it says both models should still get more done within a limit than GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol did.

Heise points out what that last claim leaves out. Part of the launch-week advantage came from the accounting error, and that part is now gone. OpenAI also hasn't said what caused the error or how many users it affected.

## Why does this matter to an estimator?

For most casual users, this is a minor annoyance. Estimators are different because they use AI in bursts. A precon lead might barely touch ChatGPT for a week, then spend bid day uploading spec sections, supplier quotes, and addenda, often with a reasoning-heavy model. That kind of usage is exactly what runs into a cap.

Per heise, the Plus plan has both a five-hour and a weekly limit in Work and Codex. Pro has no five-hour limit but does have a usage budget. A long afternoon of scope checks on Plus can hit the five-hour wall right when the bid is due. If your team used the launch week to set its habits, last week's sense of "how much I can do" is now too high.

Heise also notes that eligible Enterprise and Edu workspaces can show estimated cost per Work and Codex chat, but OpenAI's help page warns those numbers may leave out subagents, background tasks, and some tools. Treat them as a floor, not a total.

## What should a precon team do this week?

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Bid due in the next two weeks | Check Settings → Usage for your remaining allowance and reset times before the bid week starts. |
| You got a banked reset | Leave it unused. Redeem it on the web or desktop app (heise says mobile doesn't offer it) if you hit a limit on bid day. |
| Heavy spec review or quote leveling | Run it earlier in the week, not in the final hours. |
| Office tools built with Codex | Expect fewer runs per plan. Re-test long jobs before relying on them during a bid. |
| Several estimators on one Business plan | Find out who uses the most, and don't assume your allowance matches theirs. Heise says Business users get varying amounts. |

We made a related point after last week's outage: [estimators who run scope checks through AI on bid day need a plan B](/posts/2026-11-22-openai-chatgpt-api-outage-75-percent-three-and-a-half-hours-construction-estimator-bid-day-ai-steps-fallback). A usage cap fails the same way an outage does, at the worst time, and a second provider you've already tested covers both.

## What's still unclear?

More changes are coming. Heise reports that in December OpenAI plans to merge Plus, Pro and Business allowances across ChatGPT and Codex, measured in tokens, with Instant chat replies not counting against the shared budget. OpenAI hasn't said how big that budget will be. Any plan you make now covers the next few weeks, not the year.

**Takeaway:** Before your next bid, open your ChatGPT usage page and note what's left and when it resets. Move the heavy AI steps earlier in the week, and keep the banked reset unused as insurance for bid day.
